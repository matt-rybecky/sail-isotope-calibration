# comparison — this recalibration vs the ARM archived record

Intercomparison of the recalibrated 1-minute dataset (`../data/published/`)
against the ARM-archived SAIL water-vapor-isotope datastream (Galewsky WVISO,
DOI 10.5439/2280801), an independent, earlier calibration of the same Los Gatos
analyzer measurements. Both series are put on a common hourly grid and compared
per isotope. Generated by `../code/pub_arm_comparison.py`.

## Figures
- `arm_overlay_timeseries.{png,pdf}` — the two calibrations overlaid, one panel
  per isotope (this study in black, ARM in gray).
- `arm_scatter.{png,pdf}` — this study vs ARM per isotope, with the 1:1 line
  (dashed) and the Deming errors-in-both fit (gray).
- `arm_difference_timeseries.{png,pdf}` — (this study − ARM) per isotope; the
  seasonal trend is the signature of the rolling VSMOW-SLAP normalization.
- `arm_comparison_{dD,d18O,d_excess}.png` — per-isotope 2x2 diagnostics
  (scatter, Bland-Altman, difference vs time, difference vs H2O).

## Statistics
`arm_comparison_stats.{md,csv}` — bias, SD, RMSE, Pearson r, Deming
slope/intercept, Bland-Altman limits of agreement, drift per 30 days, and the
correlation of the difference with H2O, per isotope (n = 4,404 hourly bins).

Headline: dD differs by a near-constant +9.3 permil offset (flat drift, r
0.995); d18O carries a seasonal drift (+1.2 permil/30 d) with clear humidity
dependence; d-excess inherits an amplified seasonal divergence (drift
−10.2 permil/30 d, r 0.53) because it is dD − 8·d18O.

## Regenerating
The ARM record is external (cited by DOI, not shipped). Order it from the ARM
archive (DOI 10.5439/2280801) and run, from `../code/`:

```bash
python3 pub_arm_comparison.py --arm-path /path/to/ARM_wviso.dat \
    --out-dir arm_comparison_output
```

The committed figures used the ARM-archived `.dat` (columns `Time,
Mixing Ratio (g/kg), dD (permil), d18O (permil)`), mapped by the defaults in
`pub_arm_comparison.py`.
